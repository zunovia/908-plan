Voxna is a self-care app that records and analyzes how your voice changes over time.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open the app and tap the record button
2. Speak freely for 30 seconds
3. Your voice is analyzed automatically and the results are shown right away

FOUR METRICS
- Energy: the strength and vitality of your voice
- Clarity: how clearly you articulate
- Expression range: intonation and expressiveness
- Tempo: the rhythm and pace of your speech

GOOD FOR
- Reflecting on your daily condition through your voice
- Practicing speeches and presentations
- Noticing gradual changes in your voice

FEATURES
- All data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to external servers
- No account registration required (anonymous sign-in)
- Available in English, Japanese, Spanish and Portuguese
- Weekly and monthly reports to look back on changes
- AI-generated insights based on your voice patterns

A habit of facing yourself through your voice, with your privacy respected.
